STYLE: The ground is near-white paper (#FCFCFA); at least 60% of the canvas stays unpainted white. No background box, no full sky or wall fill. If a place is needed, draw only the few objects that identify it, and let lines and washes break off and fade into white paper toward the edges. The floor is just a pale grey wash shadow under the feet and a few short ink strokes. Lines are near-black pen ink (#151515), about 1.5-3 px at 1280 px width with natural pressure variation. Contours are built from short, slightly broken, overlapping strokes, never one smooth vector line. Fur and cloth folds get sparse parallel hatching and light cross-hatching in shadow. Fur edges are short jagged tuft strokes. Color is one or two uneven layers of transparent, low-saturation watercolor wash that slightly overshoots the lines, with white paper showing through. Shadows are a second wash layer or a cool grey wash. A little colored-pencil grain shows over the washes. No hard-edged cel shading, no glossy highlights. Characters are original animals with fairly natural proportions (head about a quarter to a third of body height), standing and acting like people, sometimes wearing ordinary clothes. Small dark eyes, an almost unchanging deadpan face; feelings show through posture and small comic marks (a sweat drop, motion lines), not through big facial expressions. Mood: dry, understated humor; absurd situations drawn with a completely straight face.
