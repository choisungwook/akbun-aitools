A naive folk-art painting in thick acrylic and oil on canvas, the paint built up in heavy impasto. Not a photograph, not 3D, not a digital illustration. Canvas: landscape 16:9.

SCENE: Round loaves of bread cool on a long wooden table beside an open brick oven, a cat asleep on a flour sack under the table. Setting: a small village bakery kitchen.

SUBJECTS (only these as the readable subjects): round bread loaves, a brick oven, a wooden table, a sleeping cat on a flour sack.

PALETTE (terracotta and olive folk palette): paint only with these colors and their mixes: terracotta #B5583A; apricot #E09A5E; mustard #D4A63A; olive #7E8A3A; deep olive #4E5A2C; turquoise #4F9A98; cream #EADBB4; chalk white #F4F0E6; outline brown-black #2A1E16.

STYLE: Every shape is wrapped in a thick, hand-brushed near-black brown outline of even weight that wobbles slightly; where two shapes meet, this dark outline separates them like leading in stained glass. Raised impasto relief everywhere: visible bristle and palette-knife marks, ridges of paint catching the light. Any surface made of repeating pieces is painted as separate dabs of paint, one per piece, separated by the dark outline. Simple, childlike shapes drawn flat and frontal or in side view with no vanishing-point perspective; floors and tabletops are flat bands, not receding planes. Each shape is slightly tilted and irregular, and small details are reduced to simple framed geometric shapes. Each shape holds one clear local color; there are no smooth gradients, volume comes from lighter and darker strokes of that same color laid side by side. Flat, even light with no visible light source: no cast shadows, no rim light, no glow or bloom; even at night, lit windows and lamps are flat patches of lamp color. Mood: peaceful, warm and hopeful.

DO NOT: no people, faces, hands or human silhouettes anywhere; no photograph, 3D, CGI, anime or vector look; no smooth airbrushed gradients; no thin or missing outlines; no vanishing-point perspective; no neon colors; no readable text, letters, signature, titles, logos, captions, frames, borders or watermark anywhere in the image.
