# Architecture

## Responsibility

Creates an image prompt for a quiet cream-paper pencil scene with wide negative space and one teal object.

## Boundary

Hold the restrained pencil language and emotional stillness; do not force a recurring subject or composition.

The skill wiki records maintenance context. [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) remains the executable instruction source and must not depend on an agent loading this wiki during an ordinary user request.

## Flow

Identify the emotional moment, choose a sparse scene and viewpoint, reserve negative space, then write the final image prompt.
