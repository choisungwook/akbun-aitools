# Architecture

## Responsibility

Defines only the signposted structure of a post: announce the count, use numbered blocks, and close with a recap and next action.

## Boundary

Apply this structure only when the subject naturally divides into peer reasons, steps, or items; do not impose a voice or invent filler to satisfy a count.

The skill wiki records maintenance context. [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) remains the executable instruction source and must not depend on an agent loading this wiki during an ordinary user request.

## Flow

Choose the natural block count, announce it in the introduction, write parallel numbered blocks, then recap and provide the next action.

## Resources

- [`SKILL.md`](../SKILL.md): invocation boundary and runtime instructions.
- [`references/`](../references/): supporting resources retained outside the maintenance wiki.
