# Architecture

## Responsibility

Defines only a warm Korean voice for a beginner who needs a close, experienced peer rather than an authoritative lecturer.

## Boundary

Combine haeyo-style familiarity and first-person experience with concrete facts; do not invent personal experience or alter the article's structure.

The skill wiki records maintenance context. [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) remains the executable instruction source and must not depend on an agent loading this wiki during an ordinary user request.

## Flow

Confirm the audience needs reassurance, preserve the existing structure, rewrite voice and distance consistently, and retain specific evidence.
